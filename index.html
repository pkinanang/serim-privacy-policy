01

Introduction & Scope

Welcome to the Serim Privacy Policy. Serim ("we", "our", or "the Application") is a cross-platform local peer-to-peer (P2P) file sharing solution designed for mobile devices (Android, iOS) and desktop operating systems (macOS, Windows, Linux).

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Serim to share files between devices (e.g., Phone ↔ Phone, Phone ↔ Laptop, Laptop ↔ Laptop). Our core design philosophy is Privacy by Default: all server logic runs locally on your own hardware via embedded Dart HTTP/WebSocket servers.

💡
Core Guarantee: Serim does not maintain cloud servers to relay, buffer, or analyze your files. When you transfer a file, data streams directly over your local Wi-Fi router or personal mobile hotspot.
02

Information We Never Collect

Unlike traditional cloud storage or sharing services, Serim operates without accounts, central databases, or remote telemetry. We explicitly do NOT collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal Identity: Your real name, email address, phone number, or social media logins.
  • File Contents: The names, documents, photos, videos, or raw bytes you transfer remain private between pairing devices.
  • Location Data: GPS coordinates, precise geographical location, or cell tower location identifiers.
  • Analytics & Behavioral Telemetry: How often you open Serim, how many files you send, or session duration.
  • Advertising Identifiers: IDFA, AAID, or custom advertising tokens.
03

Information Processed Locally

To establish local device discovery and facilitate high-speed transfers, Serim temporarily processes certain technical metadata in memory (RAM) and on-device storage:

  1. Local Network Address: Your device's internal IP address (e.g., 192.168.1.45) and assigned port for the local Dart HTTP server.
  2. Device Alias: A user-editable device name (e.g., "Peter's MacBook Pro") transmitted to paired devices during connection.
  3. Temporary Pairing Tokens: Cryptographically generated SHA-256 tokens embedded inside the QR code to authenticate connection handshakes.
  4. Transfer Logs: A local SQLite history database storing file names, file sizes, timestamps, and transfer completion status on your device.

05

Security & Authentication Standards

Unauthorized devices on your local network cannot access your files. Serim implements robust security controls:

  • QR Authentication Handshake: Device A displays a unique QR code containing a temporary session token and connection URL. Device B must physically scan the QR code to authenticate.
  • SHA-256 Token Validation: Every incoming HTTP request and WebSocket frame is validated against the pairing token generated during session creation.
  • Local Firewall Binding: Embedded servers bind specifically to the active Wi-Fi / Hotspot interface and do not expose ports to public WAN interfaces.
🛡️
End-to-End Local Direct Transfer: File transfers use direct TCP streams between devices without intermediary proxy nodes.
06

Data Storage & Retention

All transfer logs, device settings, and preference flags are stored locally on your device using sqflite and shared_preferences.

You retain 100% control over this data. You can clear your transfer history at any time by navigating to Serim Settings → Storage → Clear Transfer History or by uninstalling the application. Uninstalling Serim completely removes all stored database records.

08

Your Rights & Controls

Under global privacy regulations (including GDPR, CCPA, and APPs), you have the right to access, restrict, and delete your personal data. Because Serim does not retain your data on external servers:

  • Right to Erasure: You can delete all data instantly by clearing Serim's app cache or removing the app.
  • Right to Portability: All files received through Serim exist directly in standard user directories (e.g., Downloads folder) accessible by your file manager.
  • Revoking Permissions: You can revoke Camera or Network permissions anytime in your mobile/desktop system settings.

Children's Privacy

Serim does not target or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because no personal data is collected or transmitted to remote servers, Serim is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
